# Contextula Outreach Art Direction

This page is the prospect's first contact with Contextula. It must not feel like a generic SaaS template, a procedural report, or an AI page generated in thirty seconds.

It should feel like a **Victorian invitation to a powerful operator system**: bespoke, atmospheric, ceremonial, premium, and slightly dangerous — but still trustworthy and useful for a real business owner.

## Core Emotional Target

The page should make a prospect feel:

- Someone already paid attention to my business.
- This is private and prepared specifically for me.
- This company has taste and capability.
- This is not just a website builder; this is an operator that can keep improving my public presence.
- I remain safe because approvals are explicit and nothing goes live without me.

## Visual Storytelling

The page should unfold like a proposal/invitation:

1. **The Seal / Invitation**
   - Contextula appears first as a premium operator brand.
   - Use the fanged red C mark, not a generic monogram.
   - The first impression should be ceremonial: a private brief, proposal, or invitation.

2. **The Castle / Atmosphere**
   - Gothic castle/red moon/dark landscape energy is a metaphor for enduring presence, not literal horror.
   - The composition should have depth, fog, architecture, and negative space.
   - It should feel like entering a carefully controlled world.

3. **The Personalized Proposal**
   - A framed parchment/proposal module should say what was prepared for the prospect.
   - It should include concrete services/benefits, but as a tailored brief, not a checklist dump.

4. **The Preview Reveal**
   - The private preview should appear as a polished artifact inside the page.
   - The preview is proof of work already done.
   - CTA copy should feel elevated: "View your custom preview", "Open private preview", etc.

5. **Capability / Trust Section**
   - Capabilities should be shown with refined iconography and concise language.
   - Strategic design, performance, SEO/visibility, security/trust, continuous evolution.
   - This section explains Contextula's power without turning into a feature grid.

6. **The Next Step**
   - Close with a strong invitation: reply, schedule, approve next version.
   - The tone should be confident and human, like a commissioned proposal.

## Composition Requirements

- Use cinematic hierarchy: oversized serif headline, red emphasis, quiet body copy.
- Strong left/right composition with visual depth, not flat stacked cards only.
- Ornamentation should frame and elevate the content: borders, seals, filigree, wax-stamp cues.
- Use real generated imagery/art direction, not only CSS gradients and boxes.
- Avoid fake low-quality mockups, illegible dashboard text, or stock-photo SaaS devices.
- Maintain enough business clarity that a local owner understands the offer immediately.

## Brand Requirements

- Logo: red fanged C mark + Contextula wordmark.
- Palette: black/charcoal, blood red, aged ivory, muted gold/bronze accents.
- Type: elegant serif for headline/wordmark; restrained sans for operational details.
- Tagline candidates:
  - Persistent context. Continuous growth.
  - Your persistent digital operator.
  - We don't just build websites. We operate your digital presence.

## Copy Requirements

Avoid generic AI-builder copy.

Prefer:

- "A private preview, prepared for you."
- "Your public presence is often the first impression your business makes."
- "We created a tailored first pass to elevate your brand, improve performance, and give customers a clearer path to act."
- "Nothing publishes without approval."
- "Contextula remembers what matters and keeps improving from there."

Avoid:

- "AI generated website"
- "Template"
- "Fast and easy website builder"
- Generic feature-list language without atmosphere
- Overexplaining internal artifacts, critique scores, or implementation details

## Key Product Truth

The outreach page should communicate that Contextula is not selling a one-off website. It is offering a persistent digital operator for the business. The first preview is proof that the operator already started working.
